Policy group F1

The harmonic mean of policy group precision and policy group recall, computed from graded credit for recovered HIDE quasi-evidence groups.

Precision divides total policy credit by the number of predicted groups, with an additional penalty for groups containing KEEP information. Recall divides policy credit by the number of HIDE groups.
